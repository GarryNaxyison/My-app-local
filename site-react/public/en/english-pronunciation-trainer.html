Questions and answers

How can I train English pronunciation?

In NERIVA you can say a phrase, get a transcript, see weak words, and repeat a more natural version.

What is shadowing?

Shadowing means listening to a model phrase, repeating it, and reinforcing rhythm and sound.

Do I need a microphone?

Voice checks need a microphone. Text lessons and notes work without one.

Can I train phrases, not only words?

Yes. NERIVA connects words with phrases and speaking practice.

Are weak words saved?

Yes. Weak words and notes can return in later exercises.
