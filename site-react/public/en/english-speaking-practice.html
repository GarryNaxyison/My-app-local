Questions and answers

How can I practice English speaking online?

Choose a scenario in NERIVA, answer in text or voice, get a correction, and save phrases for review.

Can I practice without a teacher?

Yes. NERIVA does not replace every teacher task, but it helps you build answers regularly and notice mistakes.

What speaking topics can I practice?

Travel, work, exams, everyday small talk, cafes, hotels, airports, and other practical situations are a good fit.

Can I use voice?

Yes. Voice practice connects speaking and pronunciation in one training loop.

Is progress saved?

Yes. Mistakes, notes, and lesson history stay in one profile.
