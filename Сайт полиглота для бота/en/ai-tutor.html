How a session works

A lesson starts with a goal and a phrase in context. NERIVA asks for an answer, checks meaning, shows a natural version, and saves progress.

AI tutor vs vocabulary app

A vocabulary app helps with isolated words. NERIVA adds context: phrase, answer, correction, weak spot, note, and next review.

More than a chat

A plain chat replies to a message. NERIVA keeps a learning loop: explanation, task, check, mistake, XP, review, and progress history.

Web app and Telegram

The web app is better for longer lessons, plans, and progress. Telegram helps with quick starts, voice, reminders, and continuing the same profile.

Questions and answers

What is the NERIVA tutor?

It is a web app and Telegram workflow where AI guides a lesson, checks your answer, explains mistakes, and saves progress.

Can I start for free?

Yes. Free gives starter lessons and practice, while Premium and Platinum unlock higher limits and voice tools.

Do I need Telegram?

No. The main experience works in the web app. Telegram is an optional companion channel for quick starts and notifications.

How is it different from a vocabulary app?

NERIVA trains phrases in context and corrects answers, instead of only showing cards and translations.

Does it replace a human teacher?

No. NERIVA helps you practice independently and more often, but it does not claim to replace every role of a human teacher.
