Questions and answers

Can I study only in Telegram?

Yes, short practice is available in Telegram, while the web app is better for longer sessions and profile control.

Does progress sync?

Yes. NERIVA is designed as one learning profile across the web app and Telegram.

What can I send in Telegram?

Text, voice answers, and photos for translation or context practice.

Is Telegram required?

No. You can start from the web app. Telegram remains an optional quick channel.

Are there paid features?

Yes. Free, Premium, and Platinum differ by daily limits and access to voice and photo tools.
