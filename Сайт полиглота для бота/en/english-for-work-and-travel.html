Questions and answers

What work topics can I train?

Calls, emails, self-intros, small talk, deadlines, and clarifying questions.

Can it help before a trip?

Yes. You can rehearse hotels, cafes, airports, transport, and other daily situations.

Can I translate photos?

Yes. A photo of a menu, sign, or exercise can become a translation and practice prompt.

Are phrases saved?

Yes. Useful phrases can stay in notes and return for review.

Is Telegram required?

No. The main workflow is in the web app, while Telegram remains an optional quick channel.
