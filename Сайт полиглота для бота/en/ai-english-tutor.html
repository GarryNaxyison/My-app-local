What it is for

Speaking and pronunciation

Mistakes and review

Questions and answers

What is the NERIVA AI English tutor?

It is a web app and Telegram workflow where AI guides short English lessons, checks answers, explains mistakes, and saves progress.

Can I practice spoken English?

Yes. NERIVA gives roleplay, questions, free answers, and corrections so you build English phrases more often.

Does NERIVA help pronunciation?

Yes. Voice practice and shadowing help you repeat phrases, see weak words, and bring them back for training.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. You can start with simple phrases and move gradually into more complex situations.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The main experience works as a web app, while Telegram is an optional quick channel.
